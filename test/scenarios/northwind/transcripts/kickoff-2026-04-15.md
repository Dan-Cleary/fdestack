# Kickoff Call — Northwind Logistics

**Attendees:** Maya Chen (Northwind, Staff Eng), Raj Patel (Northwind, VP Eng), Tom Reilly (Northwind, Sec/Compliance), Dan Cleary (Anthropic FDE)

---

**Dan:** Thanks for making time. Want to start with what you're trying to accomplish in the next 60 days?

**Raj:** Top line — we want to ship a Claude-powered chatbot over our internal Confluence. Maya's team owns it. We want something employees can actually use by end of June.

**Dan:** Got it. Help me understand the urgency — what changes if you ship vs. don't ship by June?

**Raj:** [pauses] Honestly? Renewal conversations start in Q3. I told my boss we'd have a working AI product internally before then. He's bullish on Anthropic but the board is asking what we're actually getting for the money. So I need a demo.

**Maya:** And we have a real problem to solve. Our ops support team is buried. Like 40% of their tickets are "how do I update a load in the TMS" or "what's the SOP for a detention claim" — all of that is documented in Confluence but people don't read it. They Slack support instead.

**Dan:** So is the win "Maya's team ships a cool demo" or "tier-1 ticket volume goes down"?

**Maya:** Both? But if I'm being honest the second one is what makes this real. If we just ship a chatbot nobody uses, Raj has a demo but I have a maintenance burden.

**Raj:** Fair point. Let's say success is: 200+ weekly active employees and measurable deflection on the support queue by end of pilot.

**Tom:** I need to flag — before we go further — Confluence has page-level ACLs. We have legal documents, exec comp data, M&A working docs all in there. If this bot leaks anything across permission boundaries that's a fireable offense.

**Dan:** Understood. What does your current approach to that look like?

**Tom:** Right now? People just respect the ACLs because Confluence enforces them. If we put a chatbot on top we need to inherit those at query time. I don't know how that works with vector search.

**Dan:** That's a real constraint and we'll have to design around it. Question on infra — what does your stack look like?

**Maya:** Python and FastAPI for internal services, deployed on AWS us-east-1, Okta for SSO. We use Snowflake for the warehouse but nothing AI-shaped yet. People keep telling me we need a vector DB. I haven't picked one.

**Dan:** What's driving the "us-east-1 only" thing — is that a hard constraint?

**Tom:** Legal won't approve customer data crossing regions. Confluence is technically internal but it has customer data referenced throughout, so by extension yes, hard constraint.

**Dan:** OK. And deployment path — when this thing is ready, what does shipping to production look like?

**Tom:** SOC2 review. We're audited annually and any new system touching internal data goes through me. Queue is 6 weeks minimum right now.

**Maya:** Which is why I want to scope a pilot that's clearly internal/non-production for the first phase. Like, ship to 20 friendly users, prove it out, then go through SOC2 for the wider rollout.

**Raj:** That works. What do you need from us, Dan?

**Dan:** A few things. One — I'd love read access to Confluence so I can understand the shape of the data. Two — can you put me in touch with whoever runs ops support? I want to look at their actual ticket data, see what's getting asked. Three — Tom, is there an architecture pattern you've seen work for ACL-aware retrieval? I have some ideas but want to know what you'd accept.

**Tom:** I haven't seen one I love. Honestly that's the part I'm most worried about.

**Dan:** Noted. I'll come back with a proposal next week.
